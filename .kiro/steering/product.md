# Product constraints

GoLive Impact Copilot serves a clinical informatics lead preparing a software rollout and reviewing early staff feedback. Preserve this focused before/after journey. It assists review; it does not diagnose, prescribe, approve clinical deployment, execute hospital integrations, or alter patient records.

The default Northstar hospital, existing apps, EarlyWatch candidate, rollout issues and demo staff reports are fictional. Public research and historical hospital aggregates must remain visibly separate. No claims of clinical benefit, saved minutes, alert rate, hospital misuse, novelty, or vendor partnerships may be invented.

Keep `.kiro/` tracked for the Hackachusetts submission. Do not put API keys, `.env`, runtime databases, raw hospital records, or `node_modules` into Git. Record only Kiro activities that actually occurred in KIRO_USAGE.md.
