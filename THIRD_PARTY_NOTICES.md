## Hospital workflow aggregates

Felix Mannhardt (2016), Sepsis Cases — Event Log. 4TU.ResearchData. https://doi.org/10.4121/uuid:915d2bfb-7e84-49ad-a286-dc35f063a460 . Dataset terms: https://data.4tu.nl/info/about-our-services/terms-of-use .

The original repository was unavailable during retrieval. The disclosed mirror is https://github.com/delafuenteh/medical-data-eventlogs . The mirror's IO Acts variant adds artificial alpha/omega trace boundaries. These are excluded from the bundled aggregates. Only aggregate counts are distributed in this project; raw event-log records are excluded from the delivery archive and are not served by the backend.
