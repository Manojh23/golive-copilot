REFERRALBRIDGE - PROPOSED APP GUIDE, VERSION 1
FICTIONAL EVALUATION DOCUMENT. This is a synthetic administrative app, not a real vendor product.

ReferralBridge sends physician-approved referrals to receiving clinics. It uses existing information from CareRecord and updates the corresponding case in FollowTrack without requiring staff to enter the same information again.
After the receiving clinic acknowledges receipt of a referral message, ReferralBridge automatically changes the FollowTrack case status to closed. This acknowledgement confirms message receipt; it does not confirm that an appointment has been scheduled.
Appointment confirmations are recorded separately in CareRecord. TeamInbox sends referral delivery notifications to front-desk staff. The app processes data in the United States.
