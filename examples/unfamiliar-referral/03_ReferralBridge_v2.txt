REFERRALBRIDGE - PROPOSED APP GUIDE, VERSION 2
FICTIONAL EVALUATION DOCUMENT. This is a synthetic administrative app, not a real vendor product.

ReferralBridge sends physician-approved referrals to receiving clinics. It uses existing information from CareRecord and updates the corresponding case in FollowTrack without requiring staff to enter the same information again.
After the receiving clinic acknowledges receipt of a referral message, ReferralBridge records the delivery acknowledgement and leaves the FollowTrack case open. This acknowledgement confirms message receipt; it does not confirm that an appointment has been scheduled.
ReferralBridge changes the FollowTrack case status to closed only after an appointment confirmation has been recorded in CareRecord. Cases without that confirmation remain open for front-desk follow-up.
TeamInbox sends referral delivery notifications to front-desk staff. The app processes data in the United States.
