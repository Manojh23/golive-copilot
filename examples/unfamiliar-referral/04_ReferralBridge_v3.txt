REFERRALBRIDGE - PROPOSED APP GUIDE, VERSION 3
FICTIONAL EVALUATION DOCUMENT. This is a synthetic administrative app, not a real vendor product.

ReferralBridge sends physician-approved referrals to receiving clinics. It uses existing information from CareRecord and exchanges referral status information with FollowTrack without requiring staff to enter the same information again.
The receiving clinic acknowledges receipt of referral messages. This acknowledgement confirms message receipt; it does not confirm that an appointment has been scheduled.
Appointment confirmations are recorded separately in CareRecord. TeamInbox sends referral delivery notifications to front-desk staff. The app processes data in the United States.
